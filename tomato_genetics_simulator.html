Scientific limitation

This is a simplified classroom Mendelian simulation. It assumes two independent genes with complete dominance. Real tomato heat tolerance and water-use efficiency can be influenced by many genes, plant development and environmental conditions. The environmental survival score here is illustrative, not experimental evidence.
